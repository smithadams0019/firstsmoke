A lookout for mountain-top camera networks

One camera sees a weak change. Firstsmoke turns the pixel column it appeared in into a compass bearing, works out which other cameras overlook that bearing, reads them, and crosses what they answer. Pick an incident below to watch it happen.

Nothing is being watched yet

Four incidents are bundled with the service, one for each way the watch can end: a confirmed crossing, a stand-down, a fire nobody can corroborate, and a pair of cameras that are up but not watching anything.

MP4 (H.264) decodes most reliably. A zip of stills works too.

Real time from one frame to the next. A lookout camera is 60. If you leave it empty, a video is taken as 1 s a frame and stills as 60 s, and the result says so.

Footage with no surveyed camera runs on one camera. Nothing can cross its bearing, so the result is never a location, and a long clip is sampled and reported as analysed X of Y frames.
